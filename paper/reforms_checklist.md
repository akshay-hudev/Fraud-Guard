Real data: corrected resampled t-test over the overlapping rotations (Nadeau & Bengio 2003), the corrected repeated cross-validation test over 25 rotations (Bouckaert & Frank 2004), and a provider-level bootstrap with trained models fixed (§5.3, §8, S9) |
