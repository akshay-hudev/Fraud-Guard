## 3. New experimental results (all verified from run records)

* **Routing (Table 4, Fig. 5).** Re-rank routing (GB-CAG filters, GNN decides): GB-CAG→HGT ΔAP −0.001 [−0.004, 0.002],
  GB-CAG→SAGE +0.001; validation picked 5% escalation in 30/54 worlds for HGT; escalating more to HGT lowers ring AP
  (0.980 → 0.968) and isolated AP (0.626 → 0.508). Fused routing: GB-CAG→HGT +0.003 (Holm p = 0.18), GB-CAG→SAGE +0.006
  [0.004, 0.009] (Holm p < 0.001), but validation chose to fuse every claim in 22 (HGT) / 30 (SAGE) of 54 worlds:
  an ensemble effect, not routing. Hybrids: ring +0.008, isolated −0.08 to −0.11, overall ≈ GB-CAG. Ring level: every
  method put ≥ 1 claim of every test ring in the top 5% in all worlds.
* **Cost (§6.7).** One audit period (≈4,900 claims, 11,200 nodes): features 0.05 s, graph 0.01 s, scoring 0.05 s
  (GB-CAG) and 0.12 s (HGT). Per claim: GB-CAG 2.1 / 3.1 ms (p50 / p99), HGT 10.7 / 14.7 ms on its exact 3-hop
  subgraph (≈126 nodes, 1% of the graph; matches full-graph scores to 1.2e-7). Batch routing: escalating 10% touches
  55% of the graph (0.08 s vs 0.12 s). Peak RSS 470 MB.
* **HGT single-factor ablations (Table 6).** Depth 4 +0.002, heads 1/2/8 −0.028 to +0.013 (all Holm p > 0.5);
  no patient–claim edges −0.219 (p = 0.005); no claim–doctor edges −0.113 ring (p = 0.085), −0.147 isolated
  (p < 0.001); no hospital −0.002; claim attributes zeroed: ring −0.012, isolated AP → 0.036 (chance); degree
  encodings only: ring AP 0.982 ± 0.018. The earlier draft's "peak at 2 layers, over-smoothing at 3" is not supported.
* **Reliance (§7.4).** δ = 0: GB-CAG relies on aggregates for sparse rings (A −0.80, G −0.13 at s = 0.1) and on graph
  features for dense rings (G −0.81 / −0.88 at s = 0.5 / 0.9); GNNs on degree encodings (−0.76 to −0.89), not on
  attributes (< 0.05).
* **Legacy re-analysis (Table 9).** SHA-256 matches the recorded dataset hash. Ring claims: log-amount shift 1.49 s.d.,
  single-feature ROC-AUC 0.94 (benchmark δ = 2: 0.90); 21 test ring claims (3–6 per ring); 100% of test ring claims
  billed by doctors with labelled ring claims in training; GB on three raw claim attributes: ring AP 1.000, recall
  21/21 [0.84, 1.00].
* **Nested real-data tuning.** Per-rotation selection: GB-P 0.706 (shared 0.704); all tabular/MLP changes ≤ 0.010,
  none significant; best GNN (shared tuning) 0.676.
* **Holm family.** All 24 ablation comparisons now form one family; existing p-values rose slightly
  (e.g. HGT no degree encodings 0.001 → 0.003; legacy one-way graph 0.010 → 0.022); no conclusion changed.

## 5. Claims that remain unverified or need further experiments

* Online latency of a deployed system (incremental upkeep of aggregates, graph features and degree counts; storage
  retrieval; concurrency): no infrastructure exists; plan: maintain per-period counts incrementally, cache 3-hop
  neighbourhoods, and benchmark end-to-end with a replayed claim stream.
* Routing with other first stages, other GNNs, or an objective other than overall validation AP.
* HGT ablations and reliance outside δ = 0, s = 0.9 (resp. δ ∈ {0, 1, 2}); differences below ≈ 0.05 are unresolved
  with six worlds and one model seed per world.
* Nested tuning for the GNNs on the real data (≈ 20 CPU-hours).
* Ring detection on real data: no public dataset labels collusive rings.
* The earlier version's HGT +14.2 pp: not reproducible (no generating code). A run of the earlier pipeline's
  HGT on its own data was not repeated; on that data gradient boosting on three raw attributes already reaches the
  ceiling, so no model can exceed it.
* §9.3 and Table 9 present the 2,100-claim dataset as a re-analysis of a small fixed benchmark (simpler generator in
  the repository), without reference to the earlier manuscript (authors' decision, 2026-10-23).
